Insaaf AI

Audits loan, admission and hiring decisions for bias and reports in English and Urdu. It measures fairness, explains the cause with SHAP, and a mitigation agent proposes the fix. Checked against a custom Pakistani dataset and the UCI Adult Income benchmark. Built for the Alibaba Cloud AI Hackathon Pakistan 2026 with free and open-source tools only.

Shehr AI

A civic-tech referee for city cleanups. Gemini Vision compares a contractor’s claimed cleanup against citizens’ photos, GPS data from the image rules out spam, and each contractor gets a public trust score. Built at the Code for Pakistan Smart City Hackathon 2026 with two teammates.

React, Supabase, Google Gemini Vision API, Tailwind CSS, Vercel

LeadFlow AI

Qualifies inbound leads and drafts personal replies by itself. Conditional routing, human approval gates and retries let it run unattended. It cut manual lead processing by about 60% for the team using it at Tech Buzz.

n8n, OpenAI, GoHighLevel, Gmail, Google Sheets

A convolutional network trained from scratch, with no pretrained weights, that reaches 99.14% test accuracy on MNIST in 8 epochs. I studied the confusion matrix and misclassified digits, then served it through a FastAPI endpoint with a live drawing canvas that shows per-class confidence.
